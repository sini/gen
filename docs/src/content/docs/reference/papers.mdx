gen is an implementation of ideas that mostly are not its own. This page is the
bibliography: 65 papers, 51 of them
resolved to a DOI, and 24 with a
publicly readable version linked directly.

Where a paper has a summary, the citation links to it. Those summaries are our
reading of the paper — what it argues, and which part of gen it ended up
shaping — not a substitute for it.

:::note[On copies]
Only the citations and our own summaries are published here. The archive behind
this page holds PDFs and full-text transcriptions of most of these papers; those
are the publishers' to distribute, not ours, so the links go to the DOI or to an
open-access copy the authors or publisher have already made available.
:::

## Notes on this list

6 entries could not be machine-verified against a bibliographic
database and are marked above. They are recorded from the archive's own
filenames rather than from a resolved record, so treat their year and venue as
unconfirmed.

Each resolved entry was checked against the archive's own author and year before
being accepted. This matters more than it sounds: the corpus already contains a
paper that sat under the wrong year for months and was cited by the wrong title
and venue in several documents because of it, and a bibliographic search will
cheerfully return a confident, unrelated match.
